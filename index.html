Second Brain

Second Brain is a personal productivity tool built and used by one person, its developer. It helps keep track of deadlines, daily goals, notes and household chores.

What it does with Google data

  • Reads the developer's own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive, read-only, to spot upcoming deadlines and summarize what needs attention.
  • Never sends, deletes, edits or shares anything in those accounts.
  • It is not offered to the public and is used only with the developer's own Google account.
