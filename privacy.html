Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Second Brain is a personal tool used only by its developer, with the developer's own Google account. It has no other users.

Data accessed

With the developer's consent, the app requests read-only access to Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive (the gmail.readonly, calendar.readonly and drive.readonly scopes). It cannot send, modify or delete anything.

How data is used

Data is used only to remind the developer of deadlines and to organize the developer's own notes. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone.

Storage and processing

Access tokens and the notes the app creates are stored on the developer's own computer and private server, not on a public service. When the developer asks, an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) may process short excerpts to draft summaries or suggestions for the developer to review. Financial emails are only counted, never opened.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Second Brain's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The developer can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the stored token.
